======================================================================
1. PURPOSE
======================================================================

This record establishes the founding provenance of the Elias Systems
"Lock It. Log It. Prove It." technical-standard project.

The project formalises an Elias Systems discipline for establishing:

- what state was governing;
- what authority existed;
- what evidence was available;
- what determination was reached;
- what execution was attempted or completed;
- what consequence occurred;
- and what evidence supports the bounded claim subsequently made.

The discipline is expressed through three primary requirements:

LOCK IT.
LOG IT.
PROVE IT.

======================================================================
2. FOUNDING DEFINITIONS
======================================================================

LOCK IT.

Establish and bind the exact governing state relied upon before a
consequential determination or execution.

LOG IT.

Preserve the material observations, evidence, state changes,
determinations, refusals, execution attempts and consequences without
retrospectively rewriting the historical record.

PROVE IT.

Make only those claims that can be supported by the preserved evidence
and allow the bounded claim to be independently examined where the
claim requires independent verification.

======================================================================
3. FOUNDING DOCTRINES
======================================================================

CAPABILITY DOES NOT EQUAL AUTHORITY.

ATTESTATION DOES NOT EQUAL INDEPENDENT VERIFICATION.

HISTORICAL VALIDITY DOES NOT, BY ITSELF, ESTABLISH PRESENT STANDING.

RUNNABLE DOES NOT EQUAL ESTABLISHED.

TECHNICAL FEASIBILITY DOES NOT AUTOMATICALLY ESTABLISH AUTHORITY.

UNCERTAINTY IS AN ADMISSIBLE STATE.

FAILURE IS A LEGITIMATE TECHNICAL RESULT.

MATERIAL CHANGE DOES NOT RETROSPECTIVELY REWRITE HISTORY.

A MATERIAL CHANGE REQUIRES REASSESSMENT AND, WHERE REQUIRED,
A SUCCESSOR STATE OR NEW CHAIN.

NO CONSEQUENTIAL CLAIM SHALL OUTRUN THE EVIDENCE PRESERVED TO SUPPORT IT.

NO CONSEQUENTIAL EXECUTION SHALL OUTRUN THE AUTHORITY AND GOVERNING
STATE ESTABLISHED FOR IT.

======================================================================
4. CLAIM CEILING
======================================================================

Evidence establishes only the property that the evidence is capable
of establishing.

Examples:

- A matching cryptographic digest may establish byte-level identity.
  It does not establish the truth of the file contents.

- An attestation may establish that an identified party made a
  statement.
  It does not automatically establish that the statement is true.

- A successful bounded test may establish success within the tested
  conditions.
  It does not automatically establish production suitability,
  regulatory compliance or universal equivalence.

- A historical authority record may establish that authority existed
  at an earlier state.
  It does not automatically establish authority after material change.

======================================================================
5. FAILURE AND UNCERTAINTY
======================================================================

The following are legitimate bounded technical outcomes:

ALLOW
HOLD
DENY
ESCALATE
NOT ESTABLISHED
INVALID
NEW CHAIN REQUIRED

A system conforming to the LILP discipline must not manufacture a
successful outcome where required evidence, authority, identity,
standing or governing state cannot be established.

======================================================================
6. HISTORICAL PRESERVATION
======================================================================

Historical material is preserved as historical material.

Later terminology, corrections or technical improvements must not be
represented as though they existed in an earlier record when they did
not.

The Elias Systems historical semantic and symbiotic terminology will
therefore be preserved separately from successor governance and
engineering terminology while maintaining their documented lineage.

The intended lineage is:

FOUNDATIONAL
    ->
HISTORICAL
    ->
SUCCESSOR
    ->
CURRENT NORMATIVE

Where exact historical wording cannot be established from preserved
source material, that limitation must itself be recorded.
