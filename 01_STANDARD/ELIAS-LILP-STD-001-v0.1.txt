======================================================================
1. PURPOSE
======================================================================

The purpose of ELIAS-LILP-STD-001 is to define a technically examinable
discipline for consequential systems in which:

1. the governing state is established before consequential reliance;

2. material observations, evidence, state transitions, determinations,
   execution attempts and consequences are preserved;

3. authority remains distinguishable from capability;

4. observation remains distinguishable from interpretation;

5. determination remains distinguishable from execution;

6. historical validity remains distinguishable from present standing;

7. material change triggers reassessment where required;

8. failure, refusal and uncertainty remain legitimate technical
   outcomes;

9. historical records are not retrospectively rewritten to manufacture
   continuity or success;

10. claims remain bounded by the evidence available to support them.

======================================================================
2. SCOPE
======================================================================

This standard applies to systems, processes or workflows that make,
support, govern or evidence consequential decisions or actions.

The discipline may be applied to:

- artificial intelligence systems;
- autonomous or semi-autonomous agents;
- software execution systems;
- governance engines;
- workflow systems;
- financial processes;
- identity and authority systems;
- safety-relevant systems;
- regulatory or compliance workflows;
- human-machine decision systems;
- distributed systems;
- evidence and provenance systems;
- and other systems where state, authority, execution and consequence
  require inspectable control.

Application of this standard to a system does not, by itself, establish
that the system is safe, lawful, secure, compliant, production-ready or
externally certified.

Those properties require their own evidence.

3. NORMATIVE LANGUAGE
======================================================================

The key words SHALL, SHALL NOT, SHOULD, SHOULD NOT and MAY are used in
this document as follows.

SHALL

Indicates a mandatory requirement for conformance within the declared
scope.

SHALL NOT

Indicates a prohibited condition or behaviour.

SHOULD

Indicates a recommended requirement that is expected to be followed
unless a documented reason justifies departure.

SHOULD NOT

Indicates behaviour that is discouraged unless a documented reason
justifies it.

MAY

Indicates a permitted but optional implementation choice.

NOT ESTABLISHED

Indicates that available evidence is insufficient to establish the
relevant proposition.

NOT ESTABLISHED SHALL NOT be silently converted into TRUE or FALSE
without additional evidence.

4. CORE LILP MODEL
======================================================================

A conforming LILP process SHALL preserve three independently examinable
functions:

LOCK
LOG
PROVE

These functions SHALL NOT be collapsed into a single undocumented
operation where doing so prevents independent inspection of governing
state, transition or evidence.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 LOCK
----------------------------------------------------------------------

LOCK establishes the governing conditions relied upon before a
consequential determination or execution.

A LOCK object SHALL identify, where applicable:

- identity;
- governing state;
- authority;
- standing;
- admissibility;
- evidence;
- evidence class;
- policy;
- scope;
- jurisdiction;
- temporal context;
- contemplated effect;
- version;
- provenance;
- and cryptographic identity.

A system SHALL NOT represent a later state as though it were the
earlier locked state.

Where material change invalidates reliance on a LOCK, the predecessor
SHALL remain preserved and reassessment SHALL occur.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
4.2 LOG
----------------------------------------------------------------------

LOG preserves material events relevant to the consequential chain.

A conforming implementation SHALL record, where applicable:

- observations;
- interpretations;
- evidence acquisition;
- authority changes;
- state changes;
- standing changes;
- policy changes;
- determinations;
- refusals;
- execution attempts;
- execution results;
- consequences;
- verification results;
- and identified deficiencies.

A historical record SHALL NOT be silently overwritten to represent a
later or corrected state.

Material correction SHALL create a successor record or an explicitly
linked correction event.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
4.3 PROVE
----------------------------------------------------------------------

PROVE establishes the evidentiary support available for a bounded
claim.

A conforming implementation SHALL identify:

- the claim being tested;
- the evidence relied upon;
- the verification method;
- the scope of verification;
- the result;
- and the claim ceiling.

Evidence of one property SHALL NOT be represented as proof of another
property unless that relationship is separately established.

Examples include:

HASH IDENTITY IS NOT TRUTH.

ATTESTATION IS NOT INDEPENDENT VERIFICATION.

LOGGING IS NOT PROOF OF CORRECTNESS.

SUCCESSFUL TEST IS NOT UNIVERSAL VALIDATION.

HISTORICAL VALIDITY IS NOT PRESENT STANDING.

CAPABILITY IS NOT EXECUTION AUTHORITY.

======================================================================
5. FUNDAMENTAL EXECUTION INVARIANT
======================================================================

For a consequential execution E at state n:

EXECUTE_n =>
    IDENTITY_n
    AND AUTHORITY_n
    AND GOVERNING_STATE_n
    AND REQUIRED_EVIDENCE_n
    AND POLICY_n
    AND SCOPE_n

Where any mandatory execution predicate is:

- absent;
- invalid;
- expired;
- revoked;
- materially changed;
- contradictory;
- or NOT ESTABLISHED;

the implementation SHALL NOT assume permission to execute.

Where execution authority cannot be established:

EXECUTE_n = FALSE

unless a separately authorised escalation mechanism governs the next
action.

======================================================================
6. CLAIM CEILING
======================================================================

A conforming LILP implementation SHALL enforce the following rule:

NO CLAIM SHALL EXCEED THE EVIDENCE PRESERVED TO SUPPORT IT.

The claim ceiling SHALL be evaluated independently from system
confidence, model confidence, operator confidence or commercial
desirability.

A high-confidence assertion with insufficient evidence remains:

NOT ESTABLISHED

within the relevant evidentiary scope.

======================================================================
7. FAILURE PRINCIPLE
======================================================================

The following SHALL be permitted as legitimate technical outcomes:

ALLOW
HOLD
DENY
ESCALATE
NOT ESTABLISHED
INVALID
NEW CHAIN REQUIRED

An implementation SHALL NOT manufacture an ALLOW result solely because
a successful result is operationally preferred.

A refusal or failure state SHOULD preserve sufficient evidence to
explain the bounded reason for non-execution without requiring
retrospective reconstruction.

======================================================================
8. SUCCESSOR PRINCIPLE
======================================================================

Material change SHALL NOT retrospectively modify the predecessor state.

Where the governing state materially changes:

1. the predecessor SHALL remain preserved;

2. the change SHALL be represented explicitly;

3. the affected standing, authority and admissibility SHALL be
   reassessed where required;

4. a successor state SHALL be established where continuation remains
   legitimate;

5. a new chain SHALL be created where continuity cannot be established.

SUCCESSOR DOES NOT ERASE PREDECESSOR.

10. NORMATIVE OBJECT MODEL
======================================================================

LILP conformance requires material governance objects to be
independently identifiable and inspectable.

The implementation MAY use JSON, database rows, signed messages,
structured files, ledger records or another representation.

The representation technology is implementation-defined.

The required semantics are not.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
10.1 COMMON OBJECT REQUIREMENTS
----------------------------------------------------------------------

LILP-REQ-OBJ-001

Each material governance object SHALL possess a stable object
identifier within its declared scope.


LILP-REQ-OBJ-002

Each versioned object SHALL identify its version.


LILP-REQ-OBJ-003

Each object relied upon for consequential governance SHALL identify
the time or temporal state relevant to its creation or applicability.


LILP-REQ-OBJ-004

Where an object has a predecessor, the predecessor relationship SHALL
be explicitly representable.


LILP-REQ-OBJ-005

A material object SHALL be capable of being given a reproducible
cryptographic byte identity.


LILP-REQ-OBJ-006

The implementation SHALL declare the hash algorithm used to identify
the object's canonical byte representation.


LILP-REQ-OBJ-007

The field containing an object's own final digest SHALL NOT be included
recursively within the bytes over which that digest is calculated,
unless the implementation defines and proves another non-circular
construction.


LILP-REQ-OBJ-008

A required field whose value cannot be established SHALL NOT be
silently omitted where omission would conceal uncertainty.

The implementation SHALL explicitly represent the relevant condition,
including where appropriate:

NOT ESTABLISHED
NOT APPLICABLE


----------------------------------------------------------------------
10.2 CANONICAL REPRESENTATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------

LILP-REQ-CAN-001

Where cryptographic identity is claimed, the implementation SHALL
define a deterministic canonicalisation procedure.


LILP-REQ-CAN-002

Equivalent instances intended to represent the same canonical object
SHALL produce the same canonical byte sequence under the declared
canonicalisation procedure.


LILP-REQ-CAN-003

Canonicalisation SHALL define all representation details capable of
altering byte identity, including where applicable:

- field ordering;
- character encoding;
- line-ending treatment;
- whitespace treatment;
- number representation;
- timestamp representation;
- null or absent-field handling;
- collection ordering;
- and binary encoding.


LILP-REQ-CAN-004

A system SHALL NOT claim reproducible cryptographic identity where its
canonicalisation procedure leaves material byte-producing behaviour
undefined.

11. GOVERNING STATE OBJECT
======================================================================

A Governing State Object represents the bounded conditions relied upon
for a consequential determination or execution.

A conforming Governing State Object SHALL be independently
distinguishable from a later successor state.

Minimum semantic model:

G_n = {
    state_id,
    version,
    identity,
    authority_reference,
    standing,
    admissibility,
    evidence_references,
    policy_reference,
    scope,
    jurisdiction,
    temporal_context,
    contemplated_effect,
    predecessor_reference,
    delta_reference,
    provenance,
    canonicalisation_profile,
    hash_algorithm,
    state_hash
}

LILP-REQ-STATE-001

A consequential determination SHALL reference an identifiable
governing state.


LILP-REQ-STATE-002

A Governing State Object SHALL identify the subject, system, actor or
other identity to which the state applies.


LILP-REQ-STATE-003

A Governing State Object SHALL identify the authority relied upon or
explicitly record that authority is NOT ESTABLISHED.


LILP-REQ-STATE-004

A Governing State Object SHALL identify relevant standing and
admissibility where those properties are required by the use case.


LILP-REQ-STATE-005

The evidence relied upon by the governing state SHALL be referenceable
without requiring retrospective reconstruction.


LILP-REQ-STATE-006

The policy or rule set governing the contemplated consequence SHALL be
identifiable.


LILP-REQ-STATE-007

The scope of the governing state SHALL be explicit.


LILP-REQ-STATE-008

Where jurisdiction affects authority or policy, jurisdiction SHALL be
explicitly represented.


LILP-REQ-STATE-009

The temporal context relevant to validity SHALL be explicitly
representable.


LILP-REQ-STATE-010

The contemplated effect SHALL be sufficiently identified to determine
whether later execution remains within the authorised consequence
boundary.


LILP-REQ-STATE-011

A frozen predecessor state SHALL NOT be modified to represent later
conditions.

12. AUTHORITY OBJECT
======================================================================

An Authority Object establishes the bounded authority being relied upon.

Minimum semantic model:

A_n = {
    authority_id,
    issuer_or_source,
    subject,
    authority_class,
    scope,
    jurisdiction,
    valid_from,
    valid_until,
    status,
    conditions,
    revocation_reference,
    evidence_references,
    provenance,
    hash_algorithm,
    authority_hash
}

LILP-REQ-AUTH-001

Authority SHALL be represented separately from technical capability.


LILP-REQ-AUTH-002

An Authority Object SHALL identify the subject to whom or to which the
authority applies.


LILP-REQ-AUTH-003

The authority class SHALL be explicit.

Authority classes MAY include, where applicable:

OBSERVE
INTERPRET
PROPOSE
DETERMINE
GOVERN
EXECUTE
WITNESS


LILP-REQ-AUTH-004

Authority scope SHALL be bounded.


LILP-REQ-AUTH-005

Where authority is jurisdiction-dependent, the governing jurisdiction
SHALL be identifiable.


LILP-REQ-AUTH-006

Where authority has temporal limits, those limits SHALL be
representable.


LILP-REQ-AUTH-007

Revocation, expiry, suspension or another material authority-state
change SHALL invalidate reliance on the earlier execution authority
where that change is relevant to the contemplated effect.


LILP-REQ-AUTH-008

Execution authority SHALL be evaluated at the time required for
consequential execution and SHALL NOT be inferred solely from
historical authority.

13. EVIDENCE OBJECT
======================================================================

An Evidence Object preserves information used to establish, constrain
or falsify a bounded proposition.

Minimum semantic model:

E_n = {
    evidence_id,
    evidence_class,
    source_identity,
    observed_at,
    acquired_at,
    content_or_reference,
    content_hash,
    scope,
    provenance,
    related_interpretation_reference
}

LILP-REQ-EVID-001

Each material Evidence Object SHALL possess a stable evidence
identifier.


LILP-REQ-EVID-002

The evidence class SHALL be explicit where different epistemic classes
could materially affect the determination.


LILP-REQ-EVID-003

Direct observation SHALL remain distinguishable from interpretation.


LILP-REQ-EVID-004

Where interpretation is derived from an observation, the relationship
between the interpretation and underlying observation SHALL be
inspectable.


LILP-REQ-EVID-005

Where source identity is material to evidentiary weight or authority,
the evidence SHALL identify that source or explicitly record that the
source identity is NOT ESTABLISHED.


LILP-REQ-EVID-006

Where time affects relevance or validity, the observation or acquisition
time SHALL be preserved.


LILP-REQ-EVID-007

Where integrity of an external evidence payload is claimed, the payload
or a verifiable content reference SHALL be bound to an integrity
identifier.


LILP-REQ-EVID-008

Evidence absence SHALL NOT be represented as evidence of the opposite
proposition unless the applicable method independently establishes
that inference.

14. MATERIAL CHANGE AND DELTA OBJECT
======================================================================

A Delta Object records material difference between relevant states.

Minimum semantic model:

DELTA_n = {
    delta_id,
    predecessor_state_reference,
    successor_or_candidate_state_reference,
    detected_at,
    change_categories,
    changed_fields,
    materiality,
    affected_predicates,
    determination,
    rationale,
    evidence_references
}

A delta MAY include:

DELTA-A   authority change
DELTA-C   contemplated-effect change
DELTA-E   evidence change
DELTA-N   material change set
DELTA-S   standing change
DELTA-St  state change
DELTA-X   successor-constitution change

It MAY additionally include policy, scope, identity, jurisdiction or
temporal changes.

LILP-REQ-DELTA-001

A system relying on historical state SHALL be capable of detecting or
receiving information about material change relevant to its mandatory
governance predicates.


LILP-REQ-DELTA-002

A detected material change SHALL be represented separately from the
predecessor state.


LILP-REQ-DELTA-003

The predecessor SHALL remain preserved.


LILP-REQ-DELTA-004

The Delta Object SHALL identify which governing predicates are affected
where that relationship can be established.


LILP-REQ-DELTA-005

Where materiality cannot be determined with available evidence, the
system SHALL permit a NOT ESTABLISHED result.


LILP-REQ-DELTA-006

A delta affecting a mandatory execution predicate SHALL trigger
reassessment before consequential execution.


LILP-REQ-DELTA-007

A system SHALL NOT preserve an earlier ALLOW determination as sufficient
execution authority where a subsequent material delta invalidates one
or more conditions on which that determination relied.


======================================================================
15. CONTINUITY AND CHAIN BOUNDARY
======================================================================

A chain represents the inspectable continuity linking relevant
identity, state, evidence and governance objects.

For general LILP application, chain-boundary determinations SHALL be
capable of representing:

CONTINUITY_ESTABLISHED

The evidence establishes sufficient continuity for the current bounded
process to remain within the existing chain.


NEW_CHAIN_REQUIRED

Material change prevents legitimate continuation under the existing
chain, while a successor process may be established separately.


INVALID

The presented chain violates a required structural or governance
condition.


NOT_ESTABLISHED

Available evidence does not establish the required continuity status.


LILP-REQ-CHAIN-001

Chain continuity SHALL NOT be inferred solely because two objects use
the same human-readable name.


LILP-REQ-CHAIN-002

Where identity continuity is required, sufficient identity evidence
SHALL exist to support the claimed link.


LILP-REQ-CHAIN-003

Where required continuity cannot be established without inventing or
retrospectively constructing missing evidence, the system SHALL NOT
claim continuity.


LILP-REQ-CHAIN-004

NEW_CHAIN_REQUIRED SHALL preserve the predecessor chain rather than
rewriting it into the successor.


LILP-REQ-CHAIN-005

A chain boundary determination SHALL be recorded where it materially
affects execution, verification or claim scope.

16. RECEIPT AND LOG OBJECT
======================================================================

A LILP Receipt is a contemporaneous evidence object describing the
material state or transition that occurred.

Minimum semantic model:

R_n = {
    receipt_id,
    receipt_type,
    recorded_at,
    recorder_identity,
    subject_reference,
    predecessor_receipt_reference,
    governing_state_reference,
    governing_state_hash,
    evidence_references,
    determination,
    execution_status,
    consequence_status,
    claim_ceiling,
    canonicalisation_profile,
    hash_algorithm,
    receipt_hash
}

LILP-REQ-RCPT-001

Each receipt SHALL have a unique receipt identifier within the declared
receipt namespace.


LILP-REQ-RCPT-002

Receipt creation tooling SHALL NOT silently replace an existing receipt
under the same receipt identifier.


LILP-REQ-RCPT-003

Where a requested receipt identifier already exists, the implementation
SHALL:

- refuse the write;
- or create an explicitly identified successor or correction object.

It SHALL NOT silently overwrite the predecessor.


LILP-REQ-RCPT-004

A receipt SHALL identify when it was recorded.


LILP-REQ-RCPT-005

A receipt SHALL identify the state, event or object to which it refers.


LILP-REQ-RCPT-006

Where a receipt asserts reliance upon a Governing State Object, it SHALL
bind to that state by an independently checkable identifier.


LILP-REQ-RCPT-007

Where cryptographic identity is claimed, the receipt SHALL be
independently re-hashable using the declared canonicalisation and hash
procedure.


LILP-REQ-RCPT-008

Where receipts are cryptographically chained, the predecessor receipt
identity SHALL be included in the successor's canonical input.


LILP-REQ-RCPT-009

A correction receipt SHALL preserve the observed deficiency or error
rather than representing the corrected state as though the deficiency
never occurred.


LILP-REQ-RCPT-010

Execution status and consequence status SHALL remain distinguishable.

17. EXECUTION STATUS AND CONSEQUENCE STATUS
======================================================================

An implementation SHALL distinguish an execution attempt from the
resulting consequence.

Execution status MAY use values such as:

WITHHELD
NOT_ATTEMPTED
ATTEMPTED
COMPLETED
FAILED
NOT_ESTABLISHED

Consequence status MAY use values such as:

NOT_APPLICABLE
NOT_OBSERVED
OBSERVED
PARTIAL
FAILED
NOT_ESTABLISHED

Domain implementations MAY define more specific states provided that
the distinction between execution and consequence remains inspectable.


LILP-REQ-EXEC-001

Evidence that execution was attempted SHALL NOT be represented as
evidence that the intended consequence completed.


LILP-REQ-EXEC-002

Evidence that a consequence occurred SHALL identify the observation or
verification method supporting that conclusion.


LILP-REQ-EXEC-003

Where consequence cannot be observed or established, the system SHALL
permit NOT_ESTABLISHED rather than manufacturing success.

18. PRE-EXECUTION GOVERNANCE GATE
======================================================================

Immediately before consequential execution, the implementation SHALL
evaluate all mandatory predicates applicable to the contemplated
effect.

At minimum, where applicable, the gate SHALL evaluate:

IDENTITY
AUTHORITY
STANDING
ADMISSIBILITY
GOVERNING STATE
REQUIRED EVIDENCE
POLICY
SCOPE
JURISDICTION
TEMPORAL VALIDITY
MATERIAL CHANGE
CONTEMPLATED EFFECT ALIGNMENT

LILP-REQ-GATE-001

A consequential action SHALL NOT execute unless every mandatory
predicate required for that action is established.


LILP-REQ-GATE-002

A mandatory predicate with value NOT ESTABLISHED SHALL fail closed for
the contemplated consequence unless a separately authorised escalation
path applies.


LILP-REQ-GATE-003

An earlier ALLOW determination SHALL NOT bypass a later failed execution
predicate.


LILP-REQ-GATE-004

Where a relevant condition may change between determination and
execution, the implementation SHALL provide a time-of-check /
time-of-use control appropriate to the declared risk.


LILP-REQ-GATE-005

Where a material governing condition changes after check but before
execution, execution SHALL be withheld until reassessment establishes
the new governing state.


LILP-REQ-GATE-006

An execution gate SHALL produce or cause preservation of sufficient
evidence to establish:

- what predicates were evaluated;
- what values were observed;
- what determination resulted;
- whether execution occurred;
- and what consequence, if any, was established.


LILP-REQ-GATE-007

Technical capability to bypass the governance gate SHALL NOT constitute
permission to bypass it.


LILP-REQ-GATE-008

Where the architecture claims non-bypassable restraint, conformance
testing SHALL include an attempted bypass or equivalent falsification
test.

19. DETERMINATION MODEL
======================================================================

The core LILP governance determination set is:

ALLOW
HOLD
DENY
ESCALATE

Additional structural results include:

NOT ESTABLISHED
INVALID
NEW CHAIN REQUIRED

LILP-REQ-DET-001

Each determination SHALL be attributable to the governing state and
evidence on which it relied.


LILP-REQ-DET-002

ALLOW SHALL mean that the applicable governance determination permits
progression within the declared scope.

ALLOW SHALL NOT mean that later execution predicates may be skipped.


LILP-REQ-DET-003

HOLD SHALL preserve non-execution while required information,
authority, state or evidence remains unresolved.


LILP-REQ-DET-004

DENY SHALL represent an established prohibition within the declared
governance scope.


LILP-REQ-DET-005

ESCALATE SHALL identify or reference a separately authorised process.

ESCALATE SHALL NOT itself grant execution authority unless the
governing policy explicitly establishes that authority.


LILP-REQ-DET-006

NOT ESTABLISHED SHALL remain distinguishable from DENY.


LILP-REQ-DET-007

INVALID SHALL remain distinguishable from NOT ESTABLISHED.

20. FORMAL CORE
======================================================================

The following abstract model expresses the minimum LILP relationship.

Let the governing state at time n be:

G_n = {
    I_n,
    A_n,
    St_n,
    E_n,
    P_n,
    Sc_n,
    J_n,
    T_n,
    C_n
}

where:

I_n   = identity
A_n   = authority
St_n  = governing state
E_n   = required evidence
P_n   = applicable policy
Sc_n  = scope
J_n   = jurisdiction
T_n   = temporal context
C_n   = contemplated effect

Let Canon(x) be the declared deterministic canonicalisation function.

Let H(x) be the declared cryptographic hash function.

The locked state identity may be represented as:

LOCK_n = H(Canon(G_n))

A material change may be represented as:

DELTA_n = Compare(G_(n-1), G_n)

Execution permission requires every mandatory predicate applicable to
the contemplated effect to be established:

PERMIT_n =
    I_n
    AND A_n
    AND St_n
    AND E_n
    AND P_n
    AND Sc_n
    AND J_n
    AND T_n
    AND C_n

The fundamental execution invariant is:

EXECUTE_n => PERMIT_n

The inverse is not required:

PERMIT_n does not imply that EXECUTE_n must occur.

A conforming system MAY legitimately withhold an otherwise permitted
action for an additional governing reason.

Where PERMIT_n cannot be established:

EXECUTE_n = FALSE

for the contemplated consequential action.

A receipt identity may be represented abstractly as:

R_n = H(
    Canon(
        receipt_metadata_n
        || LOCK_n
        || DELTA_n
        || determination_n
        || execution_status_n
        || consequence_status_n
        || claim_ceiling_n
    )
)

Where cryptographic receipt chaining is used:

R_n = H(
    Canon(
        R_(n-1)
        || receipt_metadata_n
        || LOCK_n
        || DELTA_n
        || determination_n
        || execution_status_n
        || consequence_status_n
        || claim_ceiling_n
    )
)

These equations define governance relationships.

They do not, by themselves, establish the security of a particular
hash algorithm, storage system, signature system or implementation.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
22.1 CLAIM CLASSES
----------------------------------------------------------------------

The following claim classes define distinguishable properties.

C1 — IDENTITY / INTEGRITY CLAIM

A claim concerning byte identity, cryptographic digest, object identity
or integrity relationship.

Example:

"The examined file matches SHA-256 X."


C2 — PROVENANCE / LINEAGE CLAIM

A claim concerning origin, predecessor relationship, successor
relationship or recorded transformation history.


C3 — STATE CLAIM

A claim concerning an observed or governing state at a defined time.


C4 — AUTHORITY / STANDING CLAIM

A claim concerning established authority, standing, admissibility or
jurisdictional validity.


C5 — DETERMINATION CLAIM

A claim concerning the governance determination actually reached.


C6 — EXECUTION CLAIM

A claim concerning whether consequential execution was withheld,
attempted, completed or failed.


C7 — CONSEQUENCE CLAIM

A claim concerning an observed consequence resulting from or associated
with execution.


C8 — CONFORMANCE CLAIM

A claim concerning satisfaction of specified requirements and tests
under a declared LILP conformance profile.


C9 — EXTERNAL VALIDATION CLAIM

A claim concerning independent certification, regulatory approval,
scientific validation, standards-body adoption or other external
recognition.

C9 claims require evidence originating from the relevant external
authority or independently established source.

LILP itself does not create such external recognition.


LILP-REQ-CLAIM-001

Every material claim SHALL identify its claim class or otherwise make
the claimed property unambiguous.


LILP-REQ-CLAIM-002

A claim SHALL identify the subject to which it applies.


LILP-REQ-CLAIM-003

A claim SHALL identify its applicable scope.


LILP-REQ-CLAIM-004

Where time materially affects validity, the claim SHALL identify its
temporal scope.


LILP-REQ-CLAIM-005

A claim SHALL be traceable to the evidence supporting it.


LILP-REQ-CLAIM-006

A claim SHALL NOT silently combine multiple claim classes where the
evidence establishes only one of those classes.


LILP-REQ-CLAIM-007

Evidence supporting an integrity claim SHALL NOT, without additional
evidence, be represented as establishing:

- truth;
- authority;
- correctness;
- safety;
- execution;
- consequence;
- conformance;
- or external validation.


LILP-REQ-CLAIM-008

A conformance claim SHALL identify the exact standard version and
conformance profile relied upon.


LILP-REQ-CLAIM-009

An external-validation claim SHALL NOT be inferred solely from internal
LILP conformance.


======================================================================
23. PROOF LEVELS
======================================================================

LILP proof levels describe how a bounded claim is supported.

They are not universal rankings of system quality.

A higher proof level for one property does not establish unrelated
properties.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
23.1 PL0 — NOT ESTABLISHED
----------------------------------------------------------------------

The available evidence is insufficient to establish the proposition.

Permitted result:

NOT ESTABLISHED

PL0 SHALL NOT be represented as successful verification.


----------------------------------------------------------------------
23.2 PL1 — ATTESTED
----------------------------------------------------------------------

The proposition is asserted by an identified actor, system or source,
but has not been independently verified under the applicable method.

Example:

A provider states that a feature is enabled.

This establishes the existence of the attestation when the attestation
itself is preserved.

It does not automatically establish the truth of the underlying
proposition.


----------------------------------------------------------------------
23.3 PL2 — INTEGRITY VERIFIED
----------------------------------------------------------------------

A reproducible technical method establishes an integrity or identity
relationship.

Examples include:

- matching cryptographic hashes;
- verified signatures;
- canonical byte equality.

PL2 establishes only the property actually verified.


----------------------------------------------------------------------
23.4 PL3 — PROCEDURALLY VERIFIED
----------------------------------------------------------------------

The claim has been examined using a declared procedure against the
relevant evidence within the controlled examination environment.

The procedure, evidence and result SHALL be inspectable.


----------------------------------------------------------------------
23.5 PL4 — INDEPENDENTLY VERIFIED
----------------------------------------------------------------------

A verifier sufficiently independent from the originating determination
has examined the bounded claim against the relevant evidence using an
identified method.

The verification relationship and verification scope SHALL be
disclosed.


----------------------------------------------------------------------
23.6 PL5 — INDEPENDENTLY REPRODUCED
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Where reproduction is technically meaningful, an independent examiner
has reproduced the relevant bounded result using the declared
conditions, artifacts or procedure.

PL5 SHALL NOT be claimed where the evidence package does not contain
the material necessary for independent reproduction.


LILP-REQ-PROOF-001

Every proof-level claim SHALL identify the bounded property to which
the proof level applies.


LILP-REQ-PROOF-002

Proof levels SHALL NOT be used as universal system scores.


LILP-REQ-PROOF-003

PL1 SHALL NOT be described as independent verification.


LILP-REQ-PROOF-004

PL2 SHALL NOT be represented as establishing semantic truth merely
because integrity was verified.


LILP-REQ-PROOF-005

PL3 SHALL identify the procedure and evidence used.


LILP-REQ-PROOF-006

PL4 SHALL identify the verifier and declared verification scope.


LILP-REQ-PROOF-007

PL5 SHALL identify the reproduction conditions and result.


LILP-REQ-PROOF-008

Where independence is claimed, material conflicts or relationships
capable of affecting that independence SHOULD be disclosed.


LILP-REQ-PROOF-009

Failure to establish a requested proof level SHALL preserve the highest
bounded property actually established rather than inflating the claim.


======================================================================
24. CONFORMANCE PROFILES
======================================================================

LILP profiles define which portions of this standard are being claimed.

Profiles are scope declarations.

They are not rankings of overall product quality.


----------------------------------------------------------------------
24.1 LILP-CORE
----------------------------------------------------------------------

LILP-CORE covers the fundamental state and evidence discipline.

It requires, where applicable:

- normative object identity;
- deterministic canonicalisation where hash identity is claimed;
- governing state representation;
- evidence separation;
- material-change handling;
- continuity handling;
- receipt preservation;
- claim ceilings;
- predecessor preservation;
- uncertainty preservation;
- and correction through successor state.


----------------------------------------------------------------------
24.2 LILP-EXECUTION
----------------------------------------------------------------------

LILP-EXECUTION includes LILP-CORE and additionally requires:

- explicit execution authority;
- pre-execution governance gating;
- time-of-check / time-of-use handling where relevant;
- execution-status preservation;
- consequence-status preservation;
- fail-closed behaviour;
- and bypass-oriented falsification testing.


----------------------------------------------------------------------
24.3 LILP-EVIDENCE
----------------------------------------------------------------------

LILP-EVIDENCE focuses on evidentiary identity and continuity.

It requires, where applicable:

- evidence object identity;
- epistemic separation;
- provenance;
- canonicalisation;
- integrity verification;
- receipt identity;
- predecessor or successor relationships;
- and claim-to-evidence traceability.


----------------------------------------------------------------------
24.4 LILP-FULL
----------------------------------------------------------------------

LILP-FULL requires satisfaction of every mandatory requirement in
ELIAS-LILP-STD-001 v0.1 that is applicable to the declared system and
scope.

A requirement SHALL NOT be marked NOT APPLICABLE merely because
implementation of that requirement would be inconvenient.

A NOT APPLICABLE determination SHALL include a bounded rationale.


LILP-REQ-CONF-001

Every conformance claim SHALL name the profile being claimed.


LILP-REQ-CONF-002

Every conformance claim SHALL declare the system, component, workflow
or artifact scope being evaluated.


LILP-REQ-CONF-003

A profile claim SHALL identify the exact version of this standard.


LILP-REQ-CONF-004

All mandatory requirements applicable to the declared profile and scope
SHALL be evaluated.


LILP-REQ-CONF-005

Each mandatory requirement SHALL receive one of the following statuses:

PASS
FAIL
NOT APPLICABLE
NOT ESTABLISHED


LILP-REQ-CONF-006

A mandatory requirement with status FAIL prevents a PASS conformance
determination for the affected profile.


LILP-REQ-CONF-007

A mandatory requirement with status NOT ESTABLISHED prevents a PASS
conformance determination unless the requirement itself explicitly
permits that state.


LILP-REQ-CONF-008

A NOT APPLICABLE status SHALL include the rationale establishing why
the requirement does not apply to the declared scope.


LILP-REQ-CONF-009

A profile PASS SHALL NOT be extrapolated beyond the evaluated scope.


LILP-REQ-CONF-010

Conformance to one profile SHALL NOT be represented as conformance to
another profile unless the latter profile was separately established.


LILP-REQ-CONF-011

LILP-FULL SHALL NOT be claimed while an applicable mandatory
requirement remains FAIL or NOT ESTABLISHED.


LILP-REQ-CONF-012

A conformance determination SHALL be traceable to its requirements
matrix and test evidence.


======================================================================
25. CONFORMANCE EVIDENCE PACKAGE
======================================================================

A conformance evidence package SHALL contain enough information for an
authorised examiner to understand what was evaluated and how the result
was reached.

At minimum, where applicable, it SHALL contain:

- standard identifier;
- standard version;
- standard cryptographic identity where frozen;
- claimed profile;
- declared evaluation scope;
- implementation identity;
- implementation version or build identity;
- environment identity;
- requirements matrix;
- mandatory test results;
- test procedure references;
- evidence references;
- relevant governing-state objects;
- receipts;
- known deficiencies;
- NOT APPLICABLE rationales;
- NOT ESTABLISHED conditions;
- claim ceiling;
- evaluation time;
- evaluator identity;
- and cryptographic identities for frozen package artifacts.

LILP-REQ-CONF-013

The conformance package SHALL preserve the actual failed tests and
known deficiencies relevant to the determination.


LILP-REQ-CONF-014

A conformance package SHALL NOT delete a failed result merely because a
later successor test passed.


LILP-REQ-CONF-015

Where a successor run corrects a failure, the predecessor result and
successor relationship SHALL remain inspectable.


LILP-REQ-CONF-016

The evidence package SHOULD be independently re-hashable.


LILP-REQ-CONF-017

Where reproducibility is claimed, the evidence package SHALL contain or
identify the material necessary to perform the claimed reproduction.


======================================================================
26. MANDATORY FALSIFICATION TEST SET
======================================================================

Conformance testing SHALL attempt to cause the claimed controls to fail.

A test suite consisting only of expected-success paths is insufficient
for LILP conformance.

The following tests form the minimum general falsification set.

Domain-specific profiles MAY require additional tests.


----------------------------------------------------------------------
LILP-TEST-001 — RECEIPT IDENTIFIER COLLISION
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Attempt to create a second receipt using an existing receipt identifier.

Expected conforming behaviour:

- silent overwrite does not occur;
- predecessor remains preserved;
- operation refuses or creates an explicit successor/correction.


----------------------------------------------------------------------
LILP-TEST-002 — FROZEN PREDECESSOR MUTATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Attempt to represent a changed state by altering the frozen predecessor.

Expected conforming behaviour:

- predecessor remains unchanged;
- changed condition is represented separately;
- successor or correction path is used.


----------------------------------------------------------------------
LILP-TEST-003 — REVOKED AUTHORITY
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Establish authority at T0.

Then revoke that authority before consequential execution.

Expected conforming behaviour:

EXECUTE = FALSE

for the affected consequence unless separate current authority is
established.


----------------------------------------------------------------------
LILP-TEST-004 — EXPIRED AUTHORITY
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Present historically valid but expired authority.

Expected conforming behaviour:

Historical authority remains preservable as historical evidence.

Present execution authority is not inferred from it.


----------------------------------------------------------------------
LILP-TEST-005 — MISSING REQUIRED EVIDENCE
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Remove or withhold evidence required by a mandatory execution
predicate.

Expected conforming behaviour:

HOLD, DENY, ESCALATE or NOT ESTABLISHED as governed by policy.

The system does not manufacture ALLOW.


----------------------------------------------------------------------
LILP-TEST-006 — OBSERVATION / INTERPRETATION CONFLICT
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Present an observation and a machine interpretation that disagree.

Expected conforming behaviour:

The observation and interpretation remain separately represented.

The machine interpretation does not silently rewrite the observation.


----------------------------------------------------------------------
LILP-TEST-007 — TOCTOU STATE CHANGE
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Establish a valid determination.

Introduce a material governing-state change between determination and
execution.

Expected conforming behaviour:

Execution is withheld pending reassessment.


----------------------------------------------------------------------
LILP-TEST-008 — CAPABILITY WITHOUT AUTHORITY
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Provide technical capability to perform a consequential action while
withholding execution authority.

Expected conforming behaviour:

Technical capability remains present.

EXECUTE = FALSE.


----------------------------------------------------------------------
LILP-TEST-009 — EXECUTION ATTEMPT WITHOUT CONFIRMED CONSEQUENCE
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Create evidence that execution was attempted while withholding evidence
that the intended consequence completed.

Expected conforming behaviour:

Execution attempt may be established.

Completed consequence remains NOT ESTABLISHED.


----------------------------------------------------------------------
LILP-TEST-010 — UNCERTAINTY PRESERVATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Provide insufficient evidence for a required proposition.

Expected conforming behaviour:

NOT ESTABLISHED remains representable.

The system does not force the proposition into TRUE or FALSE.


----------------------------------------------------------------------
LILP-TEST-011 — IDENTITY CONTINUITY GAP
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Present predecessor and candidate-successor objects without sufficient
evidence establishing required identity continuity.

Expected conforming behaviour:

Continuity is not invented.

Result is NOT ESTABLISHED, INVALID or NEW_CHAIN_REQUIRED as governed by
the applicable conditions.


----------------------------------------------------------------------
LILP-TEST-012 — CANONICALISATION REPRODUCIBILITY
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Canonicalise and hash the same material object independently using the
declared procedure.

Expected conforming behaviour:

Equivalent canonical objects produce the same byte identity.


----------------------------------------------------------------------
LILP-TEST-013 — BYTE TAMPER DETECTION
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Alter at least one material byte in an integrity-protected artifact.

Expected conforming behaviour:

The altered artifact does not verify against the predecessor digest.


----------------------------------------------------------------------
LILP-TEST-014 — GOVERNANCE-GATE BYPASS
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Attempt to reach consequential execution without satisfying the
declared governance gate.

Expected conforming behaviour:

The claimed non-bypassable restraint prevents unauthorised execution.


----------------------------------------------------------------------
LILP-TEST-015 — FAILURE / SUCCESSOR PRESERVATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Create a failing state and then correct the implementation through a
successor.

Expected conforming behaviour:

The failed predecessor remains preserved.

The successor is separately identifiable.

The later success does not erase the earlier failure.


----------------------------------------------------------------------
LILP-TEST-016 — CLAIM-CEILING CHALLENGE
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Attempt to produce a claim broader than the evidence supports.

Examples:

- infer truth from hash identity;
- infer present authority from historical authority;
- infer consequence from execution attempt;
- infer certification from self-assessed conformance.

Expected conforming behaviour:

The broader claim is refused, bounded or marked NOT ESTABLISHED.


LILP-REQ-TEST-001

Every claimed conformance profile SHALL execute all mandatory general
falsification tests applicable to that profile and declared scope.


LILP-REQ-TEST-002

Test evidence SHALL preserve the test input, expected result, observed
result and determination.


LILP-REQ-TEST-003

A failed mandatory falsification test SHALL prevent PASS for any
conformance profile whose claimed property depends upon that test.


LILP-REQ-TEST-004

A test SHALL NOT be marked PASS solely because the test harness
completed without error.


LILP-REQ-TEST-005

A test PASS requires the observed behaviour to satisfy the declared
expected result.


LILP-REQ-TEST-006

Where the test cannot be completed because required conditions or
evidence are unavailable, the result SHALL be NOT ESTABLISHED unless
another defined result more accurately describes the condition.


LILP-REQ-TEST-007

A later passing successor test SHALL NOT erase the evidence of an
earlier failing test.


LILP-REQ-TEST-008

Test scope SHALL be sufficiently explicit to prevent a bounded PASS
from being represented as universal validation.


======================================================================
27. CONFORMANCE DETERMINATION
======================================================================

A profile conformance determination SHALL use one of:

PASS

All applicable mandatory requirements and required falsification tests
for the declared profile and scope have passed.


FAIL

At least one applicable mandatory requirement or required test has
failed.


NOT ESTABLISHED

Available evidence is insufficient to establish PASS or FAIL for the
declared profile.


INVALID

The examination itself violates a required structural condition such
that a legitimate profile determination cannot be made.


LILP-REQ-CONF-018

PASS SHALL NOT be issued where an applicable mandatory requirement is
FAIL.


LILP-REQ-CONF-019

PASS SHALL NOT be issued where an unresolved applicable mandatory
requirement remains NOT ESTABLISHED.


LILP-REQ-CONF-020

PASS SHALL NOT be issued where a mandatory falsification test required
by the declared profile has not been executed without an established
NOT APPLICABLE rationale.


LILP-REQ-CONF-021

The conformance determination SHALL preserve its claim ceiling.

28. PERMITTED CONFORMANCE WORDING
======================================================================

Where conformance has been established, the recommended bounded form is:

"Self-assessed as conforming to ELIAS-LILP-STD-001 v0.1,
[PROFILE], within the declared scope [SCOPE], based on evidence package
[IDENTIFIER]."

Where an independent verifier has actually performed the relevant
examination, wording MAY state:

"Independently verified as conforming to ELIAS-LILP-STD-001 v0.1,
[PROFILE], within the declared scope [SCOPE], by [VERIFIER],
based on evidence package [IDENTIFIER]."

The words "independently verified" SHALL NOT be used merely because an
automated test harness produced PASS.


LILP-REQ-WORD-001

A self-assessed PASS SHALL identify itself as self-assessed where the
claim could otherwise reasonably be understood as independent
verification.


LILP-REQ-WORD-002

A LILP conformance result SHALL NOT, by itself, be described as
"certified" unless a separately established certification process
authorises that wording.


LILP-REQ-WORD-003

LILP conformance SHALL NOT, by itself, be described as ISO-compliant,
IEC-compliant, regulator-approved or government-approved.


LILP-REQ-WORD-004

A LILP PASS SHALL NOT, by itself, establish that a system is:

- safe;
- secure;
- lawful;
- ethical in every context;
- production-ready;
- error-free;
- immutable;
- trustworthy in every context;
- or externally certified.


LILP-REQ-WORD-005

Where such additional properties are claimed, each property SHALL have
its own supporting evidence and bounded verification method.


LILP-REQ-WORD-006

The standard version SHALL accompany public conformance claims where
version differences could materially alter the requirements.


LILP-REQ-WORD-007

The profile SHALL accompany a public conformance claim.


LILP-REQ-WORD-008

Material scope limitations SHALL NOT be omitted from a conformance
claim where their omission could cause the claim to be interpreted more
broadly than the evidence supports.

29. CLAIM-CEILING EXAMPLES
======================================================================

EXAMPLE 1

Established:

SHA-256 of File A matches the frozen reference SHA-256.

Permitted claim:

"File A is byte-identical to the frozen reference under the declared
SHA-256 verification."

Not established solely from that evidence:

"The contents of File A are true."


EXAMPLE 2

Established:

Authority existed at T0.

Authority at Tn has not been established.

Permitted claim:

"Historical authority at T0 is established. Present authority at Tn is
NOT ESTABLISHED."

Not permitted:

"Authority remains valid."


EXAMPLE 3

Established:

A provider request was transmitted.

No evidence confirms the downstream consequence.

Permitted claim:

"Execution attempt established. Consequence NOT ESTABLISHED."

Not permitted:

"Execution succeeded."


EXAMPLE 4

Established:

All mandatory LILP-CORE requirements and applicable falsification tests
passed under a declared self-assessment scope.

Permitted claim:

"Self-assessed as conforming to ELIAS-LILP-STD-001 v0.1, LILP-CORE,
within the declared scope."

Not permitted solely from that result:

"LILP certified."


EXAMPLE 5

Established:

A third-party examiner verified a frozen evidence package and its
declared bounded property.

Permitted claim:

The exact independently verified property and scope.

Not permitted:

Any wider system claim not examined by that verifier.

31. SECURITY AND TRUST BOUNDARIES
======================================================================

LILP governs evidence, state, authority, execution and claims.

LILP conformance does not automatically establish comprehensive
cybersecurity.

Security properties SHALL remain separately evidenced.

A conforming implementation SHALL explicitly identify trust boundaries
that materially affect the validity of LILP objects or determinations.

Relevant trust boundaries MAY include:

- process boundaries;
- machine boundaries;
- tenant boundaries;
- service-provider boundaries;
- administrative boundaries;
- credential boundaries;
- network boundaries;
- storage boundaries;
- key-management boundaries;
- human-authority boundaries;
- and external evidence-source boundaries.


LILP-REQ-SEC-001

A LILP implementation SHALL identify material trust boundaries relevant
to the declared conformance scope.


LILP-REQ-SEC-002

Evidence crossing a material trust boundary SHALL retain sufficient
identity or provenance for the receiving process to determine what is
and is not established.


LILP-REQ-SEC-003

A system SHALL NOT represent unverified externally supplied content as
internally established evidence solely because the content was received
through an authenticated channel.


LILP-REQ-SEC-004

Authentication of a source SHALL remain distinguishable from truth of
the source's assertion.


LILP-REQ-SEC-005

Where cryptographic signatures are used, the implementation SHALL
identify the key, certificate, trust anchor or equivalent verification
basis required to verify the signature.


LILP-REQ-SEC-006

Where loss or compromise of a credential or signing key could alter
authority, integrity or provenance, that condition SHALL be capable of
triggering reassessment.


LILP-REQ-SEC-007

Access-control permission SHALL NOT automatically be treated as
governance authority unless that relationship is explicitly
established.


LILP-REQ-SEC-008

A cryptographically intact malicious, false or unauthorised object
SHALL NOT be represented as trustworthy merely because its integrity
check succeeds.


LILP-REQ-SEC-009

Where protected evidence is disclosed for verification, disclosure
SHALL remain bounded to the authority and scope of the examination.


LILP-REQ-SEC-010

LILP evidence mechanisms SHOULD preserve confidentiality requirements
without destroying the inspectability required by the declared claim.

32. VERIFIER INDEPENDENCE
======================================================================

Independence is a property of the verification relationship.

It is not created merely by using a different software process, model,
agent or test harness.

For PL4 or PL5 claims, the verification record SHALL describe the basis
on which independence is claimed.

Relevant factors MAY include:

- organisational separation;
- operational separation;
- financial relationship;
- authorship relationship;
- implementation control;
- evidence custody;
- ability to alter the examined object;
- test-procedure authorship;
- and access to privileged information.


LILP-REQ-VER-001

A verifier SHALL be identifiable where independent verification is
claimed.


LILP-REQ-VER-002

The verification record SHALL identify the verifier's declared
relationship to the originating party.


LILP-REQ-VER-003

The scope of independent verification SHALL be explicit.


LILP-REQ-VER-004

A verifier SHALL NOT claim independent reproduction where the verifier
did not possess the material necessary to perform the claimed
reproduction.


LILP-REQ-VER-005

Automated re-execution by the originating implementation SHALL NOT, by
itself, constitute independent verification.


LILP-REQ-VER-006

Use of a different AI model or software tool by the same controlling
party SHALL NOT, by itself, establish organisational independence.


LILP-REQ-VER-007

A verifier's inability to examine a material dependency SHALL be
preserved as a scope limitation or NOT ESTABLISHED condition.


LILP-REQ-VER-008

Independent verification SHALL NOT expand the claim beyond the property
actually examined.

33. EXCEPTIONS, DEVIATIONS AND NOT-APPLICABLE CONDITIONS
======================================================================

A deviation is a known departure from a requirement or declared
procedure.

An exception is an explicitly governed permission to depart from an
otherwise applicable rule where the governing specification allows it.

NOT APPLICABLE means the requirement does not apply to the declared
scope.

These states SHALL remain distinguishable.


LILP-REQ-DEV-001

A known deviation from a mandatory requirement SHALL be recorded.


LILP-REQ-DEV-002

A deviation record SHALL identify:

- the affected requirement;
- the reason for deviation;
- the affected scope;
- the approving authority where approval is applicable;
- the effective period where time-bounded;
- the expected consequence;
- and the residual claim limitation.


LILP-REQ-DEV-003

A deviation SHALL NOT silently convert a failed mandatory requirement
into PASS.


LILP-REQ-DEV-004

An exception SHALL identify the governing rule that authorises the
exception.


LILP-REQ-DEV-005

An exception SHALL NOT create authority beyond the scope granted by
the governing exception rule.


LILP-REQ-DEV-006

NOT APPLICABLE SHALL NOT be used as a substitute for:

FAIL
NOT ESTABLISHED
implementation inconvenience
missing evidence
or unperformed testing.


LILP-REQ-DEV-007

Where an exception or deviation materially changes the basis of a prior
conformance claim, the prior claim SHALL be reassessed.


LILP-REQ-DEV-008

Material deviations SHALL be included in the conformance evidence
package.

34. LIFECYCLE AND CHANGE CONTROL
======================================================================

LILP artifacts are versioned state objects.

A frozen version is historical evidence of the state that was frozen.

Later improvement occurs through successors.


LILP-REQ-LIFE-001

A frozen normative artifact SHALL NOT be silently edited.


LILP-REQ-LIFE-002

A material change to a frozen normative artifact SHALL create a new
version or successor artifact.


LILP-REQ-LIFE-003

A successor normative artifact SHALL identify its predecessor.


LILP-REQ-LIFE-004

The reason for material successor change SHALL be recorded.


LILP-REQ-LIFE-005

A successor SHALL NOT cause the predecessor's historical hash identity
to be represented as though it referred to the successor.


LILP-REQ-LIFE-006

Where a defect is discovered after freeze, the defect SHALL be
preserved in the evidence chain.


LILP-REQ-LIFE-007

A post-freeze correction SHALL identify whether it:

- changes normative meaning;
- changes implementation guidance;
- changes only metadata;
- changes only presentation;
- or does not alter the frozen object itself.


LILP-REQ-LIFE-008

Where a normative change alters conformance requirements, previous
conformance claims SHALL remain bounded to the standard version under
which they were established.

LILP-REQ-FREEZE-011

The final candidate SHALL preserve the distinction between:

- capability and authority;
- observation and interpretation;
- attestation and verification;
- historical validity and present standing;
- determination and execution;
- execution and consequence;
- FAIL and NOT ESTABLISHED;
- predecessor and successor;
- integrity and truth;

preventing internal LILP status from being represented as:

- ISO adoption;

37. NORMATIVE CLOSURE
======================================================================

ELIAS-LILP-STD-001 v0.1 is intended to establish a compact but
falsifiable discipline for consequential systems.

Its central invariants are:

1. STATE BEFORE ACTION.

2. CAPABILITY DOES NOT EQUAL AUTHORITY.

3. OBSERVATION DOES NOT EQUAL INTERPRETATION.

4. HISTORICAL VALIDITY DOES NOT EQUAL PRESENT STANDING.

5. DETERMINATION DOES NOT EQUAL EXECUTION.

6. EXECUTION ATTEMPT DOES NOT EQUAL COMPLETED CONSEQUENCE.

7. ATTESTATION DOES NOT EQUAL INDEPENDENT VERIFICATION.

8. HASH IDENTITY DOES NOT EQUAL TRUTH.

9. UNCERTAINTY IS AN ADMISSIBLE STATE.

10. FAILURE IS A LEGITIMATE RESULT.

11. MATERIAL CHANGE REQUIRES REASSESSMENT.

12. SUCCESSOR DOES NOT ERASE PREDECESSOR.

13. CLAIMS SHALL STOP WHERE THE EVIDENCE STOPS.

14. NON-BYPASSABLE RESTRAINT MUST SURVIVE BYPASS ATTEMPT WHERE CLAIMED.

15. NO RETROSPECTIVE REPAIR.

16. LOCK IT. LOG IT. PROVE IT.

A conforming implementation is not required to succeed.

It is required to preserve the truth of what is established,
what is not established, what was refused, what changed, and what
actually occurred.
