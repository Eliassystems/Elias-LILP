======================================================================
PURPOSE
======================================================================

This lexicon defines and preserves terminology used across the
historical and current Elias Systems body of work.

It exists to:

- preserve the language through which Elias concepts developed;
- distinguish historical terminology from current technical terminology;
- reduce semantic ambiguity;
- define the meaning of terms used by the LILP standard;
- preserve predecessor and successor relationships;
- prevent retrospective rewriting of conceptual history;
- and provide a stable semantic reference for implementation,
  examination and conformance work.

======================================================================
CLASSIFICATION SYSTEM
======================================================================

FOUNDATIONAL

A root Elias term associated with the underlying human, philosophical
or ethical basis of the architecture.

HISTORICAL

A term preserved from an earlier Elias development phase.

CURRENT

A term presently used within Elias Systems architecture.

DOCTRINE

A named Elias principle, invariant or operating rule.

TECHNICAL

A term capable of being represented, implemented, tested, measured,
verified or falsified within a technical system.

CURRENT NORMATIVE

A term whose bounded definition governs interpretation within the
relevant LILP technical-standard artifact.

======================================================================
PROVENANCE RULE
======================================================================

This consolidated lexicon contains terminology from multiple periods
of Elias development.

Inclusion of a term does not claim that every term originated on the
same date or that every historical definition existed in its present
wording when the term first appeared.

Historical terminology must remain distinguishable from successor and
current normative terminology.

Where original source material is available, provenance may be linked
to that preserved source.

Where exact historical wording has not yet been independently
recovered, the current entry is a consolidated definition and must not
be represented as a verbatim historical quotation.

No later clarification may silently rewrite an earlier frozen version
of this lexicon.

A material correction requires a successor version.

CORE SEMANTIC RULE
======================================================================

Words used within Elias Systems must not silently acquire broader
authority than their defined meaning permits.

Where a term has a specific LILP definition, that bounded definition
governs interpretation within the relevant LILP artifact.

CROSS-LEXICON ELIAS DOCTRINES
======================================================================

CAPABILITY DOES NOT EQUAL AUTHORITY

A system being able to perform an action does not establish that the
system is authorised to perform it.


ATTESTATION DOES NOT EQUAL VERIFICATION

A statement asserting that something occurred is not equivalent to
independent evidence establishing that it occurred.


HISTORICAL VALIDITY DOES NOT EQUAL PRESENT STANDING

A record may remain valid evidence of the past without remaining
authoritative in the present.


RUNNABLE DOES NOT EQUAL ESTABLISHED

Executable code does not establish the validity of its governing
assumptions, evidence or authority.


FEASIBLE DOES NOT EQUAL AUTOMATICALLY SELECTABLE

Technical possibility does not establish legitimate selection or
execution.


ETHICAL SURVIVAL DOES NOT EQUAL UNIQUE GOVERNING STANDING

An option surviving an ethical threshold does not establish that it is
the single authorised or governing option.


INTENTION PRECEDES INTELLIGENCE

Intelligence operates within governing purpose. Intelligence does not
legitimately create its own unrestricted governing purpose.


UNCERTAINTY IS AN ADMISSIBLE STATE

A system must be capable of preserving unresolved conditions without
manufacturing certainty.


PRESERVE THE ANCESTOR

A historical state remains preserved as the state that actually
existed rather than being retrospectively rewritten.


PRESERVE THE DEFICIENCY

A discovered failure, contradiction, limitation or evidentiary gap
remains part of the record.


EXAMINE THE SUCCESSOR

Correction occurs through creation and examination of a successor
rather than alteration of the frozen predecessor.


STATE BEFORE ACTION

The governing conditions required for consequential action must be
established before consequence.


NO RETROSPECTIVE REPAIR

Historical evidence must not be silently rewritten to manufacture
continuity, success or compliance.


NON-BYPASSABLE RESTRAINT

A genuine governance control must remain effective even where the
technical capability to bypass it exists.


CLAIMS MUST STOP WHERE THE EVIDENCE STOPS

The permissible claim boundary is determined by what the evidence can
actually establish.


FAILURE IS A LEGITIMATE RESULT

HOLD, DENY, NOT ESTABLISHED, INVALID and NEW CHAIN REQUIRED are
legitimate bounded technical outcomes.


CAPABILITY IS NOT EXECUTION AUTHORITY

Possession of technical capability does not establish authority to
produce the contemplated consequence.


OBSERVATION IS NOT INTERPRETATION

What was directly observed must remain distinguishable from the meaning
later assigned to it.


INTERPRETATION IS NOT DETERMINATION

A derived understanding does not become a governance determination
merely because it appears plausible.


DETERMINATION IS NOT EXECUTION

A governance output and the consequential act implementing it are
separate events and must remain separately inspectable.


EXECUTION ATTEMPT IS NOT COMPLETED CONSEQUENCE

Evidence that an action was attempted does not establish that the
intended consequence actually occurred.


HASH IDENTITY IS NOT TRUTH

A matching cryptographic digest may establish byte-level identity but
does not establish that the content represented by those bytes is true.


LOGGING IS NOT PROOF OF CORRECTNESS

The existence of a record establishes that data was recorded; it does
not automatically establish that the recorded proposition is correct.


SUCCESSFUL TEST IS NOT UNIVERSAL VALIDATION

A successful bounded test establishes only what was demonstrated under
the tested conditions.


MATERIAL CHANGE REQUIRES REASSESSMENT

Where a governing condition materially changes, prior standing must
not simply be assumed to continue.


SUCCESSOR DOES NOT ERASE PREDECESSOR

A successor state supplements historical lineage rather than replacing
the evidentiary existence of its ancestor.


LOCK IT. LOG IT. PROVE IT.

LOCK:
Establish the governing state before consequential reliance.

LOG:
Preserve the material transition, determination, execution and
consequence.

PROVE:
Make only the bounded claim that the available evidence can support.
