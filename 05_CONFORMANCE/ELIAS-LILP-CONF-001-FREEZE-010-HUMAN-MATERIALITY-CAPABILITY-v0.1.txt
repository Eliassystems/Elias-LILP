REVIEWER RATIONALE:
Capability is materially normative because the frozen standard expressly requires capability to remain distinguishable from authority, states that capability is not execution authority, and requires authority to be represented separately from technical capability. The term therefore directly constrains how technical ability may be interpreted and prevents a system’s ability to act from being treated as permission, admissibility, or authority to execute.
