PREDECESSOR EXISTS RULE:
When PredecessorExistence.Status is EXISTS, at least one explicit predecessor Identifier, Reference or EquivalentRepresentation must be present, a non-empty relationship Source must be present, and AttributedObjectId must equal the inspected GovernanceObject.ObjectId.

NO PREDECESSOR RULE:
When PredecessorExistence.Status is NONE, the NONE state and its Source must be explicit candidate content; mere absence of predecessor relationship evidence SHALL NOT establish NONE.

UNRESOLVED PREDECESSOR RULE:
When PredecessorExistence.Status is UNRESOLVED, or predecessor existence cannot be directly established from candidate content, the bounded determination SHALL be NOT_ESTABLISHED.

CONTRADICTION RULE:
A declared NONE state accompanied by explicit predecessor relationship evidence is contradictory and SHALL produce NOT_ESTABLISHED.

ATTRIBUTION RULE:
Where a predecessor relationship is required, it must be directly attributable to the inspected governed object and may not be attributed by filename, chronology, semantic similarity or unstated assumption.

NO-INFERENCE RULE:
The harness SHALL NOT derive predecessor existence, predecessor identity or predecessor relationship from filename, path, filesystem metadata, chronology, semantic similarity, system time, external lookup, unrelated fields, unstated assumptions or retrospective reconstruction.

INSPECTION RECORD RULE:
The harness shall generate the inspection record only from directly parsed candidate content and predicate outcomes; it shall not require or consume a pre-existing PASS or ESTABLISHED claim.

DESIGN BOUNDARY:
This design defines one forward OBJ-004 harness implementation shape only.
The harness identity, filename, language and invocation parameter are selected by this design but no executable artifact is created.
The harness will inspect only the designated candidate content and its SHA-256 identity.

The harness will produce structured inspection evidence to standard output and will not mutate the designated candidate.
The implementation itself is not constructed by this design review.
