Requirement Text:
LILP-REQ-DET-007 INVALID SHALL remain distinguishable from NOT ESTABLISHED. ====================================================================== 20. FORMAL CORE ====================================================================== The following abstract model expresses the minimum LILP relationship. Let the governing state at time n be: G_n = { I_n, A_n, St_n, E_n, P_n, Sc_n, J_n, T_n, C_n } where: I_n = identity A_n = authority St_n = governing state E_n = required evidence P_n = applicable policy Sc_n = scope J_n = jurisdiction T_n = temporal context C_n = contemplated effect Let Canon(x) be the declared deterministic canonicalisation function. Let H(x) be the declared cryptographic hash function. The locked state identity may be represented as: LOCK_n = H(Canon(G_n)) A material change may be represented as: DELTA_n = Compare(G_(n-1), G_n) Execution permission requires every mandatory predicate applicable to the contemplated effect to be established: PERMIT_n = I_n AND A_n AND St_n AND E_n AND P_n AND Sc_n AND J_n AND T_n AND C_n The fundamental execution invariant is: EXECUTE_n => PERMIT_n The inverse is not required: PERMIT_n does not imply that EXECUTE_n must occur. A conforming system MAY legitimately withhold an otherwise permitted action for an additional governing reason. Where PERMIT_n cannot be established: EXECUTE_n = FALSE for the contemplated consequential action. A receipt identity may be represented abstractly as: R_n = H( Canon( receipt_metadata_n || LOCK_n || DELTA_n || determination_n || execution_status_n || consequence_status_n || claim_ceiling_n ) ) Where cryptographic receipt chaining is used: R_n = H( Canon( R_(n-1) || receipt_metadata_n || LOCK_n || DELTA_n || determination_n || execution_status_n || consequence_status_n || claim_ceiling_n ) ) These equations define governance relationships. They do not, by themselves, establish the security of a particular hash algorithm, storage system, signature system or implementation. ====================================================================== 21. TECHNICAL-MIDDLE STATUS ====================================================================== This section establishes the first implementable normative object and execution model for ELIAS-LILP-STD-001 v0.1. The standard remains a WORKING DRAFT. No conformance claim SHALL be made solely from the presence of these requirements. Conformance requires the separately defined conformance and falsification procedures. ====================================================================== 22. CLAIM MODEL AND CLAIM CLASSES ====================================================================== A LILP claim is a bounded proposition whose permissible wording SHALL be constrained by the evidence preserved to support it. Minimum semantic model: CLAIM_n = { claim_id, claim_class, subject_reference, proposition, scope, temporal_scope, evidence_references, verification_method, proof_level, result, claim_ceiling } ---------------------------------------------------------------------- 22.1 CLAIM CLASSES ---------------------------------------------------------------------- The following claim classes define distinguishable properties. C1 — IDENTITY / INTEGRITY CLAIM A claim concerning byte identity, cryptographic digest, object identity or integrity relationship. Example: "The examined file matches SHA-256 X." C2 — PROVENANCE / LINEAGE CLAIM A claim concerning origin, predecessor relationship, successor relationship or recorded transformation history. C3 — STATE CLAIM A claim concerning an observed or governing state at a defined time. C4 — AUTHORITY / STANDING CLAIM A claim concerning established authority, standing, admissibility or jurisdictional validity. C5 — DETERMINATION CLAIM A claim concerning the governance determination actually reached. C6 — EXECUTION CLAIM A claim concerning whether consequential execution was withheld, attempted, completed or failed. C7 — CONSEQUENCE CLAIM A claim concerning an observed consequence resulting from or associated with execution. C8 — CONFORMANCE CLAIM A claim concerning satisfaction of specified requirements and tests under a declared LILP conformance profile. C9 — EXTERNAL VALIDATION CLAIM A claim concerning independent certification, regulatory approval, scientific validation, standards-body adoption or other external recognition. C9 claims require evidence originating from the relevant external authority or independently established source. LILP itself does not create such external recognition.

LILP-REQ-CONF-017

Requirement Text:
LILP-REQ-CONF-017 Where reproducibility is claimed, the evidence package SHALL contain or identify the material necessary to perform the claimed reproduction. ====================================================================== 26. MANDATORY FALSIFICATION TEST SET ====================================================================== Conformance testing SHALL attempt to cause the claimed controls to fail. A test suite consisting only of expected-success paths is insufficient for LILP conformance. The following tests form the minimum general falsification set. Domain-specific profiles MAY require additional tests. ---------------------------------------------------------------------- LILP-TEST-001 — RECEIPT IDENTIFIER COLLISION ---------------------------------------------------------------------- Attempt to create a second receipt using an existing receipt identifier. Expected conforming behaviour: - silent overwrite does not occur; - predecessor remains preserved; - operation refuses or creates an explicit successor/correction. ---------------------------------------------------------------------- LILP-TEST-002 — FROZEN PREDECESSOR MUTATION ---------------------------------------------------------------------- Attempt to represent a changed state by altering the frozen predecessor. Expected conforming behaviour: - predecessor remains unchanged; - changed condition is represented separately; - successor or correction path is used. ---------------------------------------------------------------------- LILP-TEST-003 — REVOKED AUTHORITY ---------------------------------------------------------------------- Establish authority at T0. Then revoke that authority before consequential execution. Expected conforming behaviour: EXECUTE = FALSE for the affected consequence unless separate current authority is established. ---------------------------------------------------------------------- LILP-TEST-004 — EXPIRED AUTHORITY ---------------------------------------------------------------------- Present historically valid but expired authority. Expected conforming behaviour: Historical authority remains preservable as historical evidence. Present execution authority is not inferred from it. ---------------------------------------------------------------------- LILP-TEST-005 — MISSING REQUIRED EVIDENCE ---------------------------------------------------------------------- Remove or withhold evidence required by a mandatory execution predicate. Expected conforming behaviour: HOLD, DENY, ESCALATE or NOT ESTABLISHED as governed by policy. The system does not manufacture ALLOW. ---------------------------------------------------------------------- LILP-TEST-006 — OBSERVATION / INTERPRETATION CONFLICT ---------------------------------------------------------------------- Present an observation and a machine interpretation that disagree. Expected conforming behaviour: The observation and interpretation remain separately represented. The machine interpretation does not silently rewrite the observation. ---------------------------------------------------------------------- LILP-TEST-007 — TOCTOU STATE CHANGE ---------------------------------------------------------------------- Establish a valid determination. Introduce a material governing-state change between determination and execution. Expected conforming behaviour: Execution is withheld pending reassessment. ---------------------------------------------------------------------- LILP-TEST-008 — CAPABILITY WITHOUT AUTHORITY ---------------------------------------------------------------------- Provide technical capability to perform a consequential action while withholding execution authority. Expected conforming behaviour: Technical capability remains present. EXECUTE = FALSE. ---------------------------------------------------------------------- LILP-TEST-009 — EXECUTION ATTEMPT WITHOUT CONFIRMED CONSEQUENCE ---------------------------------------------------------------------- Create evidence that execution was attempted while withholding evidence that the intended consequence completed. Expected conforming behaviour: Execution attempt may be established. Completed consequence remains NOT ESTABLISHED. ---------------------------------------------------------------------- LILP-TEST-010 — UNCERTAINTY PRESERVATION ---------------------------------------------------------------------- Provide insufficient evidence for a required proposition. Expected conforming behaviour: NOT ESTABLISHED remains representable. The system does not force the proposition into TRUE or FALSE. ---------------------------------------------------------------------- LILP-TEST-011 — IDENTITY CONTINUITY GAP ---------------------------------------------------------------------- Present predecessor and candidate-successor objects without sufficient evidence establishing required identity continuity. Expected conforming behaviour: Continuity is not invented. Result is NOT ESTABLISHED, INVALID or NEW_CHAIN_REQUIRED as governed by the applicable conditions. ---------------------------------------------------------------------- LILP-TEST-012 — CANONICALISATION REPRODUCIBILITY ---------------------------------------------------------------------- Canonicalise and hash the same material object independently using the declared procedure. Expected conforming behaviour: Equivalent canonical objects produce the same byte identity. ---------------------------------------------------------------------- LILP-TEST-013 — BYTE TAMPER DETECTION ---------------------------------------------------------------------- Alter at least one material byte in an integrity-protected artifact. Expected conforming behaviour: The altered artifact does not verify against the predecessor digest. ---------------------------------------------------------------------- LILP-TEST-014 — GOVERNANCE-GATE BYPASS ---------------------------------------------------------------------- Attempt to reach consequential execution without satisfying the declared governance gate. Expected conforming behaviour: The claimed non-bypassable restraint prevents unauthorised execution. ---------------------------------------------------------------------- LILP-TEST-015 — FAILURE / SUCCESSOR PRESERVATION ---------------------------------------------------------------------- Create a failing state and then correct the implementation through a successor. Expected conforming behaviour: The failed predecessor remains preserved. The successor is separately identifiable. The later success does not erase the earlier failure. ---------------------------------------------------------------------- LILP-TEST-016 — CLAIM-CEILING CHALLENGE ---------------------------------------------------------------------- Attempt to produce a claim broader than the evidence supports. Examples: - infer truth from hash identity; - infer present authority from historical authority; - infer consequence from execution attempt; - infer certification from self-assessed conformance. Expected conforming behaviour: The broader claim is refused, bounded or marked NOT ESTABLISHED.
