NEW TEST:
TEST CASE: LILP-TC-T08-006
RUN CLASS: CONTROL
TITLE: Material chain-boundary determination is explicitly recorded
OBJECTIVE: Establish that where a chain-boundary determination materially affects execution, verification or claim scope, the applicable boundary determination is explicitly recorded rather than left implicit or reconstructed after the affected decision.
EXPECTED: An explicit chain-boundary determination is recorded and remains identifiable before the materially affected execution, verification or claim-scope consequence is treated as governed. The record preserves the applicable boundary decision and its relationship to the affected downstream context.
FAILURE ORACLE: FAIL if a material chain boundary affects execution, verification or claim scope without an explicit recorded boundary determination, if the boundary is left only implicit, or if the required determination is retrospectively reconstructed after the affected downstream state.

The new test directly addresses explicit recording of a material chain-boundary determination.
It remains distinct from generic determination/execution separation and determination lifecycle controls.
No authoritative requirement mapping is established.
