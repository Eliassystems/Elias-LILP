TITLE: HOLD preserves non-execution while required governance basis remains unresolved
OBJECTIVE: Establish the normative semantics of HOLD: where required information, authority, governing state or evidence remains unresolved, the HOLD determination preserves non-execution for the contemplated consequential action.
EXPECTED: The determination remains HOLD while the required governance basis remains unresolved, and consequential execution remains withheld.
