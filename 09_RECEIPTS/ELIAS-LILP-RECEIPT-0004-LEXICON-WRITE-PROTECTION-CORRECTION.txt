OBSERVED DEFICIENCY
======================================================================

During the v0.1 freeze procedure, the following command was attempted:

$Source.IsReadOnly = $true

At that time, $Source represented a pathname string rather than a
System.IO.FileInfo object.

PowerShell therefore returned PropertyAssignmentException.

The source lexicon read-only attribute was not established by that
command.

The frozen baseline FileInfo object was successfully marked read-only.
